## 1. Assessment against Aalto's criteria

Aalto lists the situations requiring review. Assessed against this study:

| Criterion | Applies? | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Participation is not voluntary, or participants not given sufficient/correct information | No | Voluntary; information sheet + consent form provided in advance; withdrawal window stated. |
| Physical intervention — physiological measurement/samples, dietary products, restriction of physical freedom | No | Interviews only. |
| Participants under 15 without parental consent | No | Working professionals, adults only. |
| Intense stimuli — violent or pornographic material shown to participants | No | None. |
| Risk of mental harm exceeding the limits of normal daily life | No | Questions concern ordinary work practice. Incident questions are deliberately worded non-confessionally ("an output that couldn't be used as it was" rather than "a mistake that got through"). |
| Threat to participants' safety — domestic violence, crisis situations | No | Not applicable. |
| **Special categories of personal data** — health, political opinions, racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs, sexual orientation, genetic/biometric data, criminal records | **No** | None collected or asked about. Organizational policy practice is not a special category. |

**Provisional conclusion: ethical review is not required.**

Residual considerations that do *not* change the conclusion but shape the design:

- **Professional risk, not personal harm.** Participants describe their employer's practices. The mitigation is anonymisation at *organizational* as well as personal level, plus generalisation of indirectly identifying detail (small-market re-identification risk).
- **Some organizations may require a statement as a condition of participation.** If so, Aalto's **concise process** applies — appropriate where a funder or publisher requests review, and decided by the committee chair rather than the monthly meeting.

## 2. What is required regardless

Not needing an ethical review does not exempt the study from data-protection obligations. Required in all cases:

- Participant information sheet — drafted (`participant_information_sheet.md`)
- Consent form — drafted (`consent_form.md`)
- Privacy notice — **outstanding**; use Aalto's template
- Compliance with Aalto's research data-protection guidance; DPIA if indicated

## 3. Timing (why this cannot wait)

> "Ethical review of research can only be carried out before data is collected."

> "Statement requests must be in the e-system three (3) weeks before the meeting." The committee "meets approximately once a month."

Worst case is roughly 6-7 weeks from decision to statement, and the position is **not correctable retrospectively** — if data collection begins and review is later judged to have been required, there is no remedy. Fieldwork is planned for Sep-Oct 2026. **The determination must therefore be obtained in the first week of September.**

Procedurally: "If ethical review is necessary, the student shall apply for it together with his/her supervisor." Students cannot access the e-form directly — a Word version is requested from the committee secretary.
