# Before our interview: what to have in mind

**English below · 中文在下方**

---

## English

Thank you for taking part. You don't need to prepare anything in writing. It helps most if you think of **one concrete situation** from your own work before we meet, because we will spend most of the interview on it rather than on policy in the abstract.

### Pick one situation of any of these three kinds

1. **A refused or restricted use.** Someone wanted to use a generative AI tool for something in a report or analysis, and it wasn't allowed, or it had to be done differently.
2. **An output that couldn't be used as it was.** A figure, a summary, a query, or a piece of commentary that turned out to be wrong, unverifiable, or needed rework before it could go into a deliverable.
3. **A use that got approved.** A tool or a way of working that went from "someone was trying it" to "this is how we do it."

It can be your own experience or something you saw close up in your team. Ordinary, everyday examples are just as useful as unusual ones. If you have examples of more than one kind, that's welcome; one is enough.

### Things it may help to recall (roughly is fine)

- What happened, which kind of report, dashboard or analysis it was, and who was involved
- Whether there was a rule or guidance at the time, and how you knew about it
- What happened next, and whether anything changed afterwards

**You don't need to look up exact dates, documents or policies.** "Sometime last spring" or "I'm not sure" are perfectly good answers.

### Please leave out

- Anything confidential or covered by an agreement with your employer; you can skip any question without giving a reason
- Names of colleagues, clients or internal systems; describe them by role ("my manager", "a client team", "our BI tool")
- Internal documents: please don't send me any. If you ticked the document permission on the consent form, we can look at one together in a separate, optional call of about 10 minutes (screen-share or describe it). Nothing is copied, and short passages are quoted only if you also ticked that permission

### Practical

- About 45–60 minutes on Microsoft Teams, in English or Taiwanese Mandarin
- A quiet place where you can talk freely
- There are no right or wrong answers, and I'm not assessing you or your organisation

Questions beforehand: yu-shu.liu@aalto.fi

## 中文（繁體）

謝謝你願意參加。訪談前不需要準備任何書面資料。最有幫助的是，在我們見面之前，先想好**一個你工作中具體的情境**，因為訪談大部分的時間會放在這個情境上，而不是抽象地談政策。

### 從以下三種情況中挑一個

1. **被拒絕或受限的使用。** 有人想把生成式 AI 用在某份報表或某個分析上，但不被允許，或是必須換一種做法。
2. **沒辦法直接使用的產出。** 某個產出——一個數字、一段摘要、一段 query，或一段說明文字——沒辦法直接使用，需要重做或再確認過，才能放進要交出去的成果裡。
3. **後來被核准的用法。** 某個工具或某種做法，從「有人在試試看」變成「我們現在都這樣做」。

可以是你自己的經驗，也可以是你在團隊中近距離看到的事情。平常、日常的例子和特別的例子一樣有幫助。如果有不只一種類型的例子也很歡迎，但一個就夠了。

### 可以先回想的事情（大概就好）

- 發生了什麼事、是哪一類報表、dashboard 或分析、有誰參與
- 當時有沒有相關的規定或指引，你是怎麼知道的
- 後來怎麼處理，之後有沒有什麼改變

**不需要去查確切的日期、文件或政策。**「大概去年春天」或「我不太確定」都是很好的回答。

### 請不要提到

- 任何機密或受保密協議約束的內容；任何問題都可以直接跳過，不需要說明原因
- 同事、客戶或內部系統的名稱；用角色來描述就好（例如「我的主管」、「一個客戶團隊」、「我們的 BI 工具」）
- 內部文件：請不要寄給我。如果你在同意書中勾選了展示文件的選項，我們可以另外約一次約 10 分鐘的簡短通話，一起看文件（分享螢幕或口頭描述都可以），完全是自由選擇。文件不會被複製；只有在你也勾選引用選項時，才會引用簡短段落

### 訪談安排

- 約 45–60 分鐘，使用 Microsoft Teams，可以用中文或英文
- 請找一個可以安心說話的安靜地方
- 沒有標準答案，我也不是在評估你或你的公司

有任何問題，歡迎事先來信：yu-shu.liu@aalto.fi
