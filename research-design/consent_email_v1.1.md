## EN

> **Subject:** Consent form for our interview on [DATE]

Dear [NAME],

Thank you again for agreeing to take part in my master's thesis study. I've attached the consent form. It explains what the interview involves, how your data is stored and anonymised, and how you can withdraw. Please read in particular the section "Use of third-party and AI software": only a transcript with your name and other identifying details removed may be processed with such tools, and the recording stays in Microsoft Teams.

You can give your consent in either of two ways:

1. Sign the form and send it back to me; or
2. Reply to this email with the text below, answering Yes or No to each item.

You can take part while answering No to any of the six items.

> I have received and understood the consent form, and I agree to take part in this interview study.
> 1. The interview may be audio-recorded (if not, the researcher takes written notes): Yes / No
> 2. Anonymised direct quotations from my interview may be used in the thesis: Yes / No
> 3. I may show internal documents (for example an AI use policy) during the interview, only so that dates and wording are accurate. They will not be copied, quoted or reproduced: Yes / No
> 4. Short, anonymised passages from those documents may be quoted in the thesis: Yes / No
> 5. I would like a short summary of what the researcher understood from my interview, so that I can correct any misunderstanding: Yes / No
> 6. I would like a summary of the findings when the thesis is complete: Yes / No

One reminder: you take part in a personal capacity, not for your employer. Please skip anything confidential or covered by an agreement with your employer, and I won't ask why.

Our interview is on [DATE] at [TIME] [TIME ZONE] on Microsoft Teams: [LINK]. It takes about 60–75 minutes. If you have any questions about the form, just reply to this email.

1. 在同意書上簽名後回傳給我；或

> 5. 我希望收到研究者對我訪談內容理解的簡短摘要，以便更正任何誤解：是／否
