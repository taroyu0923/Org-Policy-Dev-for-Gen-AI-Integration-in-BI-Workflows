## 2. Purpose of processing

Your personal data is processed only to carry out this master's thesis research: to arrange and conduct one interview, to analyse what you describe about how rules for generative AI use reach and affect analytical work in your organisation, and to handle any withdrawal or correction request. It is not used for any other purpose, and it is not used to make decisions about you.

## 3. Legal basis

**Your consent** (EU General Data Protection Regulation, Art. 6(1)(a)). You may withdraw consent at any time up to [DATE — 4 weeks after your interview, as in the information sheet], without giving a reason and without consequence. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

## 4. Personal data processed

| Data | Why | Kept separately from the transcript? |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address | Arranging the interview; withdrawal; sending summaries if you asked for them | Yes |
| Job role, organisation, approximate tenure | Describing cases by role type, sector and tier | Recorded as generalised attributes only |
| Audio recording (if you agree) | Accurate transcription | — deleted after transcription and checking |
| Interview transcript or notes | Analysis | Pseudonymised: names, employer, team, product names and exact dates removed or generalised |
| Signed consent form | Evidence of consent | Yes |

**No special categories of personal data** (health, political opinions, religious beliefs, ethnic origin, sexual orientation, biometric data, criminal records) are asked about or collected. If you mention any in passing, it will be removed from the transcript.

Information about your **employer's practices** is not your personal data, but because it could identify you indirectly, it is generalised in the same way (see the information sheet, "Will what I say be confidential?").

## 5. Source of the data

Directly from you: the interview, the consent form and our email correspondence. Nothing is collected about you from your employer or any other source.

## 6. Who can see the data

- **Me** (the researcher): all data.
- **My supervisor**: anonymised excerpts only, for guidance and quality checking.
- **Thesis examiners and readers of the published thesis**: anonymised findings and, only if you agree (consent item 7), anonymised quotations.

No data is sold, shared with your employer, or disclosed to any other party.

## 7. Services used to process the data

| Step | Service | Where the data is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Interview, recording and transcription | Microsoft Teams (built-in recording and transcription), through my Aalto University account | Aalto University's Microsoft 365 environment (Aalto OneDrive) |
| Storage of recordings, transcripts, consent forms | Aalto OneDrive | Aalto University's Microsoft 365 environment |

These are Aalto-provided services covered by the university's agreements with the provider. Apart from Teams' built-in transcription, **no AI-based service is used to process your recording or any transcript that could identify you.** Only fully anonymised material — with names, organisations, teams, products and dates removed — may be worked on with other software tools during analysis.

Your data is stored only in Aalto University's Microsoft 365 environment and is not passed to any other party. Participants may be located outside the EU/EEA; taking part in a remote interview does not transfer your data to any third party.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict its processing; and withdraw consent (§3). Because the data is anonymised after the withdrawal date and merged into the analysis, erasure of your individual contribution is possible **until [DATE]**, not after.

To use any of these rights, contact me at [AALTO EMAIL].

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland this is the **Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman** (tietosuoja.fi). You may also contact the authority in the country where you live or work.
