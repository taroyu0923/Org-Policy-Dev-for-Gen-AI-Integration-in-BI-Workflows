# Recruitment Templates

**Version:** 0.95 (draft), Sep 2, 2026. Complete all [BRACKETED] fields before sending.

> ⚠ **Route selection (Sep 2, after the participant list was confirmed).** This sample is a professional network of individuals, several of whom are likely bound by employer NDAs (JPMorgan, Nordea, Amazon among the likeliest). **Template A is the default route for all 14 participants.** Do NOT send template B unless a participant themselves says organizational sign-off is needed — approaching an employer unprompted risks both the interview and the participant's position. Template C (referral) matters most for Shopee participants #3 and #4, who are the only realistic route to a policy owner. See `sampling_frame.md` §7.
Do not send before the ethical-review determination is in hand (see `ethics_determination_note.md`) — several organizations will ask for it.

## A. First contact — individual (cold or light warm intro)

> **Subject:** Master's research interview — how organizations set rules for AI in reporting work (60 min)

Dear [NAME],

I'm a master's student at Aalto University, researching how organizations develop and change their internal rules for using generative AI in reporting and analytics work.

Most of the published research looks at government regulation and at frameworks companies publish externally. Almost none of it looks at how these rules actually come about inside an organization, how they reach the people producing reports, and how they change once they meet real deadlines. That's the gap I'm trying to fill, and it's why I'd value your perspective.

Concretely I'm asking for one remote interview of about 60 minutes, at a time that suits you. It's a conversation rather than a questionnaire — mostly about one or two concrete situations you've experienced, and how things have changed since.

Neither you nor your organization would be named. You'd be described by role type and sector only, and I'd check anything I quote with you first. Participation is voluntary and you can withdraw afterwards. I've attached the information sheet and consent form with the full detail.

Would you be open to a conversation? If it's easier, I'm happy to answer questions by email first.

With thanks,
Liu Yu-Shu (Albert)
[PROGRAMME], Aalto University
Supervisor: [NAME], [EMAIL]

## B. Gatekeeper / organizational approach

> **Subject:** Request: research access for a master's thesis on AI governance in analytics teams

Dear [NAME],

I'm writing to ask whether [ORGANIZATION] would be willing to take part in academic research at Aalto University on how organizations develop internal policy for generative AI use in reporting and analytics.

What I'd be asking for:

- **Interviews** with two or three colleagues at different levels — for example someone who set or approved the policy, someone who manages a team applying it, and someone doing the analytical work. About 60 minutes each, remote.
- **Optionally**, the chance to look at the relevant internal guidance during those conversations, purely to be accurate about dates and wording. Documents would not be copied, quoted or reproduced, and the thesis would only record that documents were consulted. There is a separate, optional permission if you were ever willing to allow short quotations — most organizations grant the first and not the second, and the first is enough.

Neither the organization nor any individual would be named. The organization would be described by sector and approximate size. I'd be glad to share the relevant thesis sections with you before submission.

[IF APPLICABLE: The study has been assessed under Aalto University's research ethics procedures — I can provide that documentation.]

I've attached the participant information sheet and consent form. I'd welcome a short call to answer any questions.

With thanks,
Liu Yu-Shu (Albert)

Supervisor: [NAME], [EMAIL]
