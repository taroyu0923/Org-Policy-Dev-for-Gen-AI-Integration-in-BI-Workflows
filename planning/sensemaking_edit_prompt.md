## The idea in one line

The typology describes **what** governance reaches the practitioner. Sensemaking (G4, G5) describes **how** the practitioner reads an ambiguous rule and acts on it. Sensegiving (G6, G10) describes **what is sent back up**. Change-recipient research (G7, G9) shows that this reading happens mostly **laterally, among peers**, outside the control of the rule-setters.
