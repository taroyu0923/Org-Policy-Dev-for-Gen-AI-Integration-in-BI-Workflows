## Spine

The chapter follows the loop the findings are expected to test, then ends on the lens and the gap (the funnel's end):

```
rule arrives ─► unclear case ─► FORK ─► ask ─► ruling / intermediary ─► goes up?
   (2.1)          (2.2)         (2.3)  │         (2.4)                    (2.5)
                                       └─► act now ─► own judgement ─► nothing up
                                                                      ──► lens & gap (2.6)
```

⚠ The loop is a **sensitising structure**, not a finding. Headings are written as the questions the literature answers (or fails to), so the chapter survives if the data breaks the loop. Clusters A–G are source pools, not sections.
