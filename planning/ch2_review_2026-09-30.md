## Changes to make

1. **Citation form (both sections).** The author is named in the sentence *and* in the parenthetical, so pandoc renders "Mäntymäki et al. call it '…' (Mäntymäki et al., 2022, p. 604)". Use narrative citations: `@mantymaki2022definingaigov [p. 604] call it "…"` → "Mäntymäki et al. (2022, p. 604) call it '…'". Where two sources share a surname, give the year: **Papagiannidis et al. (2023)** (`papagiannidis2023towardaigov`) vs **Papagiannidis et al. (2025)** (`responsibleaigovreview`). 2.6 ¶1–2 use both without years.
2. **Definition of "rule" (2.1 ¶1).** "any statement by the organisation" excludes unwritten norms and tool blocks. The RQ covers rules "written or unwritten", and the codebook counts rulings, tool blocks and colleagues as sources of the working rule. Suggested: *"In this study, a rule is any expectation, written or unwritten, about what BI practitioners may or may not do with generative AI, however it reaches them: a document, a ruling on a request, a blocked tool or a colleague's advice."*
3. **2.1 ¶2 reads as a catalogue** (eight sources in sequence). Group them: general governance (Mäntymäki, Abraham, Lu, Ismail) → GenAI frameworks that state the cascade outright (Joshi, Lee) → practitioners as the object (Batool, Birkstedt). Same sources, fewer "X says… Y says…" transitions.
4. **Secondary source (2.1 ¶2).** "the parameters are set by legislation, organizational norms and clients" is Birkstedt et al. summarising Orr and Davis (2020). Mark it: "…as Birkstedt et al. summarise Orr and Davis (2020)".
5. **B9 tally dependency (2.6 ¶2).** "22 to 24 of 36 (61–67%)" assumes B9 counts as positive. **Resolved Sep 30: Albert confirmed B9 positive — no change to 2.6.**
6. **Forward dependency (2.6 ¶4).** "the distinction 2.3 left open" commits 2.3 to raising the before-rule / around-rule question. Logged for the 2.3 session.
