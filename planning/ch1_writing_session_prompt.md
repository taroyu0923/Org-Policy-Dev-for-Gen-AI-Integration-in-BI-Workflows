## Argument and content rules

- **No interview data, no findings, no hypotheses as findings.** Fieldwork is Oct 5–19. Anything about the sample size actually achieved gets `[FILL: …]`; anything not yet done gets `[PENDING: …]`.
- **No organisation names**, no participant titles. Describe the sample as in 3.2: 14 BI practitioners, nine organisations, five countries, two depth cases and seven breadth cases.
- **Do not duplicate** Chapter 2 (claims, sources, the tally argument) or Chapter 3 (design detail). Cross-reference by section number.
- Keep the **contribution modest and conditional** ("this study aims to…"). The null on SQ2 is a possible finding.
- **Why governance, not adoption** (open examiner question): answer briefly in 1.5. Adoption studies ask whether people use the tool; this study asks how rules shape use that is already happening or wanted.

## Style

As the Chapter 2/3 briefs: academic register, UK spelling, claim first, no throat-clearing, at most two em dashes per page, avoid delve / crucial / pivotal / landscape / tapestry / "it is important to note" / "plays a vital role". **Voice:** "this study" by default; "I" only for researcher decisions (as in Chapter 3). Define a term once and keep it (BI practitioners, rule, generative AI / GenAI: spell out at first use).
