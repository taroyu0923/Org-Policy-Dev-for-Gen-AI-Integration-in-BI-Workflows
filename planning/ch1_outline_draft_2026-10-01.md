## 1. The thesis at a glance

**Working title (proposed):** *Governance Seen from Below: How BI Practitioners Encounter, Interpret and Act on Their Organisations' Rules for Generative AI*

Other candidates:
1. *Rules from Above, Practice from Below: A Qualitative Multiple-Case Study of Generative AI Rules in Business Intelligence Work*
2. *Receiving the Rules: BI Practitioners and Organisational Governance of Generative AI*
3. *Does Anything Go Up? BI Practitioners' Encounters with Generative AI Rules and the Routes Back to Rule-Makers*

The current registered title, *Organizational Policy Development for Generative AI Integration in Business Intelligence Workflows: A Qualitative Analysis of Governance Framework Evolution*, promises policy development and framework evolution. The research question no longer does: no participant writes policy, and evolution is reported only if a threshold fixed before the interviews is met (Section 3.4). "Evolution" can return to the subtitle after the analysis if the evidence supports it.

**Research question (RQ v2.1, settled):**
- **Main:** How do BI practitioners encounter, interpret and act on their organisation's rules for generative AI use?
- **SQ1 (downward):** What happens when they use, or try to use, GenAI in their analytical work, and how do the rules shape what they do next?
- **SQ2 (upward):** Do their requests or concerns travel upward to whoever decides the rules, and what comes back?

**Summary.** Organisations have answered generative AI with rules: documents, approval steps, blocked tools, rulings on individual requests. The governance literature describes how such rules are designed and passed down, but it rarely reports how the people who receive them experience them; 28 or 29 of the 36 governance sources screened contain no such account (Section 2.6), and none takes business intelligence (BI) work as its unit of analysis. This study interviews 14 BI practitioners in nine organisations in five countries, anchoring each account in specific incidents of using or trying to use generative AI. It asks what the rules did to their work, what they did next, and whether anything they raised travelled back to those who set the rules. A finding that nothing travels upward is a possible and reportable answer to SQ2.
