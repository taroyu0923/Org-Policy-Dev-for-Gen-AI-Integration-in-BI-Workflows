**Version:** 0 (Sep 29, 2026, before the pilot) | **Status:** starting frame; expected to change after the pilot and the Shopee interviews
**Lives in:** repo (`analysis/templates/codebook_v0.md`) — definitions only. **Examples are anonymised paraphrases only** (no names, organisations, teams, products, dates or verbatim quotes), so this file may be worked on with AI tools. > ⚠ **Rule (protocol §10): no a priori code may be the answer to the RQ.** The spine and seed codes organise the data; the findings come from the inductive codes and from where the data departs from the spine and the loop.

> ✅ **Label decided (Sep 30, 2026): codebook thematic analysis** (Braun & Clarke 2021, `braun2021onesize` — see the decision block in `literature/notes/M2_braun2021_one_size_fits_all.md`). ## C. Spine — governance practices (a priori)

Working definitions adapted from D1 Papagiannidis et al. (2025) pp.10–13, A12 Tallon et al. (2013) Table 6 and E2 Abraham et al. (2019) §4.1. **Check wording against D1 before quoting in Chapter 3.**

| Code | Working definition (as the practitioner encounters it) | Include | Exclude |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STR — structural** | Who holds decision rights over GenAI use: roles, committees, owners, approval points | "Regional decides", named approvers, a governance team | How the decision was made (→ PRO) |
| **PRO — procedural** | Written or built-in ways the rules operate: policies, approval processes, approved-tool lists, blocks, data restrictions, review before publishing | Policy documents, blocked sites, request forms | Informal "we just know" (→ REL) |
| **REL — relational** | How rules move between people: communication, training, asking colleagues, escalation, informal norms, who you ask when unsure | Peer advice, local BI screening, training sessions, shared norms | The formal existence of a process (→ PRO) |

## E. Sensitising lens — not codes

Used to order analysis and memo-writing, not to label segments. Report where the data breaks it.

```
unclear case → FORK (ask / act now / lateral) → ruling or own judgement → working rule → up or not
```

**Sensemaking (second lens, added Oct 2, 2026; Chapter 2.6, Chapter 3.4):** a sensitising concept, as in Balasooriya & Sedera (2026, p. 7923). It guides the memos (cue, plausible reading, who it was talked over with, sensegiving; familiarisation memo §3a and §4) and **adds no codes or themes**. Sources: G4–G10.

Claims to hold the data against (from the claims map): K1 tier cascade · K2 formal channels · K5 bypass from over-control · K7 feedback loops · K9 intermediaries · K11 governance first · P2 bypass as continued habit.
