## 1.6 Intended contribution and thesis structure

The study aims to contribute in three ways. First, it aims to provide accounts of receiving generative AI rules from the BI practitioners who receive them, traced through specific incidents rather than surveyed or reported by executives. Second, it aims to establish whether anything practitioners raise travels upward to those who set the rules; that nothing does is a possible answer. Third, it aims to apply the structural, procedural and relational lens from the receiving end, where relational practices are likely to be the ones practitioners can see most directly.

Chapter 2 reviews the literature by following the path a rule is assumed to take, from its arrival through unclear cases, bypass and rulings to the question of whether anything goes up, and ends with the analytical lens and the gap. Chapter 3 sets out the research design, sampling, data collection, analysis, trustworthiness, ethics and the method of the literature review. Chapter 4 reports the findings within each case and then across cases, keeping the depth and breadth strata apart. Chapter 5 discusses the findings against Chapter 2 and the lens, and states the study's limitations. Chapter 6 answers the research questions and sets out the contribution, the implications for organisations and directions for further research.
