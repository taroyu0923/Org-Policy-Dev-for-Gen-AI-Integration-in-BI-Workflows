## 1. WHY
The paper takes stock of sensemaking and restates it as a process of organizing: how people notice, label and act on ambiguous cues, and how that produces and sustains organization. *Location: opening text, pp.409–410.*

## 2. HOW
Conceptual stock-taking essay. No sample, data collection or corpus; it synthesises organisation-theory work and draws on cases reported by others (for example Snook's friendly-fire analysis, cited from p.410). *Location: pp.409–410.*

## 3. WHAT
- **Seven properties of sensemaking, as the paper's section headings:** noticing and bracketing (p.411), labeling (p.411), retrospective (p.411), presumption (p.412), social and systemic (p.412), action (p.412), organizing through communication (p.413). (NotebookLM said "eight properties"; the paper does not.)
- **Sensemaking as intraorganizational evolution** (p.413–414): an ecological change–enactment–selection–retention sequence with retention feeding back to the earlier processes.
- **Instigations to sensemaking** (p.414): breached expectations of continuity.
- **Plausibility** (p.415), **identity** (p.416), and a prospective agenda: institutional theory and distributed sensemaking (p.417), power and emotion (p.418).

## 4. DEFINITION
"Sensemaking involves turning circumstances into a situation that is comprehended explicitly in words and that serves as a springboard into action." *(p.409)*
"Sensemaking involves the ongoing retrospective development of plausible images that rationalize what people are doing." *(p.409)*
"Viewed as a significant process of organizing, sensemaking unfolds as a sequence in which people concerned with identity in the social context of other actors engage ongoing circumstances from which they extract cues and make plausible sense retrospectively, while enacting more or less order into those ongoing circumstances." *(p.409)*

## 5. CITABLE
The paper does not address AI or analytical work. Candidates for Chapter 2 §2.6:
- "Categories have plasticity because they are socially defined, because they have to be adapted to local circumstances, and because they have a radial structure" and, on peripheral cases, "their action is more variable, more indeterminate, more likely to alter organizing, and more consequential for adapting" *(p.411)*

- "If we conceptualize organizing as a sequence of ecological change-enactment-selection-retention with the results of retention feeding back to all three prior processes, then the specific activities of sensemaking fit neatly into this more general progression of organizing" *(p.414)*
- "Sensemaking is not about truth and getting it right. Instead, it is about continued redrafting of an emerging story so that it becomes more comprehensive, incorporates more of the observed data, and is more resilient in the face of criticism" *(p.415)*

## 13. THESIS ANCHOR (v2.4-T; focus: definition of sensemaking; cues, ambiguity and plausibility; how sensemaking turns into action; the link between organizing and sensemaking)
- **Definitions:** §4.
- **Main claims:** "Sensemaking is about the interplay of action and interpretation rather than the influence of evaluation on choice. When action is the central focus, interpretation, not choice, is the core phenomenon" *(p.409)*. - **Mechanisms and conditions:** breached continuity (p.414), noticing and bracketing and labeling (p.411), retrospection (p.411), presumption and action (p.412), communication (p.413), identity (p.416), power (p.418).
