## 1. WHY
AI integration into corporate governance reshapes decision-making and creates operational advantages, but introduces "legal gray areas" around transparency, liability, and data privacy. The paper is motivated by the need to bridge technological advancement and legal/governance accountability.

## 3. WHAT
- "Encapsulated trust" — redefines the fiduciary relationship between human stewards and AI systems. *(p.124)*
- Shareholder communication improved by AI chatbots vs. "black box" AI proxy voting risk. *(Section 4, pp.125–127)*
- Liability allocation via "operator's liability" and contractual agreements. *(pp.125–127)*
- Algorithmic bias in AI risk assessment — eliminates cognitive bias but risks perpetuating historical bias. *(Section 5, pp.128–130)*
- Legal personality of AI as central determinant of IP ownership/civil liability. *(Section 5)*
- Six-part strategic governance framework: transparency, data governance, ethics training, regular audits, legal collaboration, AI-based compliance tracking. *(Section 6.2, p.131)*

## 4. DEFINITION
