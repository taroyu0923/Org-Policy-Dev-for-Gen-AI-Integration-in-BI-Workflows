## 1. WHY
AI governance is tech-deterministic/inward-focused; GenAI creates opaque "smog" (hallucinations, "Data Dysphoria"), a "responsibility gap"; rigid IT controls fail for complex socio-technical systems. *Location: Abstract p.38; Intro pp.38-39; Sec. "GenAI in practice" pp.42-43.*

## 2. HOW
Theoretical/conceptual framework paper. No empirical sample; synthesizes IT governance, AI ethics, public administration, CAS theory, and industry surveys (Blackberry 2023, CapGemini 2024). *Location: Abstract p.38; Intro pp.38-39; Sec. "AI governance conceptualized as CASs" pp.40-41.*

## 3. WHAT
- CAS (Complex Adaptive Systems) conceptualization of GenAI. *(pp.40-41)*
- Six IT-governance-streams taxonomy. *(pp.41-42)*
- Seven-cause opaqueness/failure-mode typology. *(pp.42-43)*
- Governance mechanisms matrix (5 categories × 4 levels). *(pp.43-45)*
- Shift from predictive to joint accountability. *(pp.43-44)*
- Three-stage evolutionary model: Project → IT → Responsible governance. *(p.45, Table 2)*
- Seven guiding principles for responsible governance. *(pp.46-47)*

## 4. DEFINITION
"Responsible governance can be defined as joint efforts between stakeholders to establish oversight mechanisms based on societal values and norms, where societal concerns and the complexity of AI governance are taken as a starting point, and where people and society are engaged and empowered." *(p.46)* Also: "Responsible governance is a move from an inward organizational perspective to an outward organizational perspective on governance." *(p.39)*

## 5. CITABLE
- "GenAI effectively serves as cognitive scaffolding, aiding staff in performing a task at a level that it would otherwise be unable to attain." *(Sec. "GenAI in practice", p.42)*
- "Bottom-up processes can result in unintended or unforeseeable consequences on individual-level behaviors." *(Intro, p.38)*; also: "CAS theory suggests that influencing the behavior of individual agents can influence the system as a whole by creating new patterns." *(Intro, p.39)*
- "Once humans use GenAI, the system will learn from usage, resulting in feedback loops. Mistakes can also be corrected and fed back into the system using feedback loops. As the system evolves, governance should also evolve..." *(Sec. "GenAI governance mechanisms", p.43)*
