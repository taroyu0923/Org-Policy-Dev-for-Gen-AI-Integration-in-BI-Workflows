## 7. INTERVIEW VALUE
The paper contains no pre-packaged interview instruments, but its evaluated frameworks map directly onto a semi-structured interview design across three phases:

**Phase 1 — Policy Framework Development**
- Strategic Alignment (NTT DATA Strategy Tier): how executives/policy designers define the boundary between BI optimization and ethical risk mitigation (Section 4.3, p. 129).
- Participatory Co-Governance (Barus et al. Co-Design Model): level of involvement of business analysts/frontline BI users in shaping GenAI acceptable-use policies (Section 3.4, p. 127 & Section 4.2, p. 128).
- Anticipatory Risk Assessment (Xue & Pang Design Tier): how organizations anticipate "ethical debt" and identify generative-model risks (e.g., training-data bias) prior to deploying BI pipelines (Section 4.2, p. 128).

**Phase 2 — Implementation & Change Management**
- Three Lines of Defense (Hong Kong SAR Ethical AI Framework): accountability boundaries across developers, business managers, internal auditors when deploying automated BI reports (Section 4.1, p. 128).
- Organizational Enablement (NTT DATA Organization Tier): operational friction, educational hurdles, role of internal ethics boards ("AI hubs") in managing GenAI-tool deployment (Section 4.3, p. 129).
- Technical Policy Safeguards (Ligot's risk mitigations): how policies dictate data sanitization, LLM prompt-engineering constraints, and data security in live BI queries — via federated learning and prompt checks (Section 6.4, pp. 133–134).

**Phase 3 — Organizational Adaptation Dynamics**
- Continuous Compliance Auditing (NTT DATA Lifecycle Management Tier): how policies adapt to post-deployment model drift, hallucinations in BI decision-support systems, ongoing auditability of automated insights (Section 4.3, p. 129).
- Dynamic Adaptation to Emergent Risk (Xue & Pang Monitoring Tier): how organizations structurally handle policy revisions when generative BI engines produce anomalous or politically sensitive outputs (Section 4.2, pp. 128–129).
- Labor Market & Institutional Reskilling (Saba & Ngepah Governance Model): planning for BI-workforce shifts (e.g., data analysts transitioning to prompt engineers/validators) and managing displacement anxiety (Section 4.3, p. 129 & Section 5.5, p. 132).
