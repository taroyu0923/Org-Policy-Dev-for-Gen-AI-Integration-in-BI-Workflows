## 1. WHY
Organizations struggle to operationalize high-level ethical AI principles/regulatory mandates (GDPR, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001) across enterprise workflows; current solutions lack interoperability, adaptability, real-time oversight → fragmented, high-risk governance (Section 1.1, p. 78–79; Section 1.3, p. 79).

## 2. HOW
SLR with PRISMA 2020 + qualitative thematic synthesis (Section 3.2–3.3, p. 80–81). 250 databases + 10 registers searched; 2019–2025 span; 260 records → 180 screened → 52 assessed → **17 reports (15 distinct studies)** in final synthesis (Section 3.1–3.3, p. 80–81).

## 3. WHAT
- Theme 1 — Architectural frameworks: layered/modular/cloud-integrated, reusable design patterns, automated compliance, data provenance, aligned to ISO/IEC 42001 & NIST AI RMF (Section 4.1, p. 81–82).
- Theme 2 — Effectiveness/ethical assurance: embedding governance in design improves transparency/compliance but efficacy limited by voluntary self-regulation (Section 4.2, p. 82; Section 5, p. 82).
- Theme 3 — Implementation barriers: interoperability deficits, stakeholder misalignment, governance-supply-chain fragmentation, sector-specific bureaucratic rigidity (Section 4.3, p. 82; Section 6.1, p. 83).

## 4. DEFINITION
