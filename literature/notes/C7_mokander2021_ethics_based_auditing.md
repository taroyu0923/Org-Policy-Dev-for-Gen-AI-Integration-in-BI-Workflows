## 1. WHY
ADMS present opacity/bias/accountability risks unresolved by traditional human governance; gap between high-level ethics principles ("what/why") and daily engineering practice ("how"). *Location: Intro pp.2-4.*

## 2. HOW
Systematised literature review + conceptual synthesis. 5 databases (Google Scholar, Scopus, SSRN, WoS, arXiv). Keywords combining auditing/evaluation/assessment + ethics/fairness/transparency/robust + automated decision-making/AI/algorithms. 2011–2021. 122 articles/reports analyzed + 16 EBA frameworks/tools evaluated. *Location: Appendix B, pp.22-23.*

## 3. WHAT
- EBA conceptualized as a formal soft post-compliance governance mechanism. *(pp.4-5)*
- Audit-type typology: functionality/code/impact audits. *(p.7, p.12)*
- Framework-origin typology: top-down vs. bottom-up. *(pp.9-10)*
- 8-step audit process synthesis. *(p.10)*
- Six methodological advantages. *(pp.13-14)*
- Seven success criteria: Holistic, Traceable, Accountable, Strategic, Dialectic, Continuous, Driving re-design. *(pp.15-16)*
- Constraint typology: conceptual/technical/economic-social/organizational-institutional. *(pp.17-20)*

## 4. DEFINITION
"we define EBA as a structured process whereby an entity's present or past behaviour is assessed for consistency with relevant principles or norms." *(Abstract p.1; Sec. Scope p.4)*

## 5. CITABLE
Primarily executive/standards-setting side, but offers practitioner-interpretation claims.
- "...interviews with software developers indicate that while they consider ethics important in principle, they also view it as an impractical construct that is distant from the issues they face in daily work" *(Sec. Intro, p.3)*
- "...to ensure that ADMS are ethically-sound, organisational policies need to be broken down into tasks for which individual agents can be held accountable" *(Sec. Criteria, p.15)*
- "Continuous monitoring and assessment ensures that a constant flow of feedback concerning the ethical behaviour of ADMS is worked into the next iteration of their design and application." *(Sec. Connecting the Dots, p.12; see also Criterion 7 "Driving re-design", p.16)*
