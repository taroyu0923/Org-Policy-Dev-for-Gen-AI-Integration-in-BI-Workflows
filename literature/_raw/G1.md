#### 1. **WHY — Research Problem and Motivation**
Rapid advancements in accessible cloud and personal IT drive employees to deploy unauthorized digital tools ("shadow IT") to boost efficiency, causing CIOs to lose control over corporate IT landscapes, increasing security/compliance risks, and degrading data analytics quality [1]. Existing literature on bring-your-own (BYO), workarounds, and IT consumerization overlaps with shadow IT but fails to address its unique characteristics [1, 2]. Consequently, the paper seeks to disentangle shadow IT from adjacent constructs and present a multidimensional research agenda covering its causes, usage patterns, consequences, and governance strategies [1-3].

* **Location**: Section 1 "1 Shadow IT – An Increasingly Relevant Phenomenon" (p. 469) [1]; Section 2 "2 Delineating the Shadow IT Concept" (pp. 469–470) [1, 2]; Section 3 "3 Shadow IT – State-of-the-Art" (p. 471) [3].
