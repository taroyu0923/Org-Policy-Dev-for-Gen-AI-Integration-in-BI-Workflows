   - Policy Encounter & Interpretation: framework explicitly links data-governance principles (accountability, transparency, quality) to AI-specific trust requirements (Sec.3, Framework); proposes governance "arrangements" as the point where organizational policy is operationalized into data/AI practice (Sec.3.2).
