7. INTERVIEW VALUE: Empirical multi-case study with a full interview protocol appendix (p.139) — HIGH VALUE, actual quoted interview items across all 3 phases:
   - Policy Encounter & Interpretation: "Do you follow any best practices for Trustworthy AI? If yes, which one? If not, why not?"; "Have you established any governance practices? For example, have you defined roles and responsibilities?"; "What types of data do you collect? How do you ensure to use data and AI algorithms such that they are in line with your organizational objectives?"; "Who is in charge of the data management and what were the requirements for that position?" (Appendix, p.139)
   - Implementation & Change Mgmt: "Are there any procedures or processes for managing the data you use in your organization (for AI purposes)?"; "How are your organization's models audited for security or privacy vulnerabilities?"; "What development practices do you follow as a team?"; "Did you try to incorporate external AI software?" (Appendix, p.139)
   - Organizational Adaptation Dynamics: "How did (i) the use of AI grow over time, (ii) how did the AI team grow over time (iii) how did the value/effectiveness of AI grow over time?"; "Are there any changes brought by AI that you did not anticipate?"; "Do you specify, monitor and evaluate the (i) behavior and (ii) outcomes of your AI systems and potentially the combination with human decision-makers? What actions are taken upon this?" (Appendix, p.139)
