* **Problem**: Although AI is increasingly promoted to assist organizations, its integration into sustainability efforts remains limited, sporadic, and ad-hoc [1]. * **Motivation**: Grounded in organizational sensemaking theory, this study addresses this empirical and academic gap by examining how successful manufacturing organizations strategically integrate AI to reshape operational values, workforce well-being, and long-term environmental accountability [1, 3, 4].
