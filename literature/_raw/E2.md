7. INTERVIEW VALUE: Moderate-High — structured literature review producing a conceptual data-governance framework (antecedents/mechanisms/consequences), no original interviews but a strong construct vocabulary directly transferable to Albert's protocol:
   - Policy Encounter & Interpretation: framework distinguishes governance "mechanisms" (structural, procedural, relational) as the concrete forms through which policy reaches staff (Sec.4, Framework); structural mechanisms (roles, committees) vs. procedural mechanisms (standards, processes) vs. relational mechanisms (collaboration, communication) map directly onto interview probes about how policy is encountered.
   - Implementation & Change Mgmt: review identifies "contingency factors" (org size, industry, data culture maturity) moderating how governance mechanisms are actually implemented (Sec.4.3); explicit research-agenda call for more studies on "the actual behavior of individuals" in data-governance implementation (Sec.5, Research Agenda) — directly supports the need for Albert's qualitative study.
   - Organizational Adaptation Dynamics: relational mechanisms (collaboration/communication) framed as the primary lever for adapting governance over time (Sec.4.2); review notes an "under-researched" gap in longitudinal/processual studies of governance evolution (Sec.5).
