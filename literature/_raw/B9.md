#### 1. **WHY — Research Problem & Motivation**
The widespread adoption of generative AI tools within organizations has sparked "Shadow AI"—the unauthorized use of AI solutions that bypass formal IT governance [1]. While inheriting risks from traditional Shadow IT, Shadow AI's autonomous, opaque, and generative nature introduces heightened threats including data privacy violations, algorithmic bias, hallucinations, and governance drift [1, 2]. Existing rigid IT control policies fail to keep pace with employee adoption, creating a sociotechnical governance failure where organizational rules lag behind day-to-day work practices [1, 2].
